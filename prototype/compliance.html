Frameworks that share your work

Frameworks overlap enormously. Compliance maps that overlap for you, so adding a framework is a delta — not a second program.

  • Ships with the full catalogs: ISO 27001/9001/14001/42001, SOC 2, GDPR, EU AI Act, Cyber Essentials
  • Crosswalk mappings connect requirements across catalogs
  • Applicability and coverage show exactly what is left to do
03

An AI assistant that does the legwork

On every page, an assistant that knows your records — not a chatbot bolted on the side.

  • Ask it anything about your ISMS; it queries your actual registers
  • It drafts policies, files risks and updates records — with your confirmation on anything destructive
  • Import your existing documentation set and let it map what you already have

Tasks that keep you certified

Certification is not a project — it is a rhythm. The task layer turns every recurring obligation into scheduled, owned work.

  • Recurring cadences for reviews, tests and training
  • Owners and due dates across every register
  • Templates for the work you do every cycle
