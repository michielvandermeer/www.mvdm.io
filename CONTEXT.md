# mvdm.io Website

The public marketing website for mvdmio. Its job is to sell the mvdmio products; everything else on it is secondary.

## Language

**Product**:
A SaaS application mvdmio sells: Compliance, Translation Tools, Health Check, Statistics, or Commonplace. Auth is not a Product — it is internal SSO plumbing.
_Avoid_: App, project, tool

**Framework**:
A billed standards line inside Compliance, such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. One Framework can cover more than one catalog edition.
_Avoid_: Product, licence

**AI credit**:
A US-dollar allowance for the Compliance assistant. Each Framework includes a yearly amount, and those amounts add when an organisation buys more than one Framework. Extra credit is bought in USD packs. Each use is the provider's list price plus ten percent, named in the Terms of Service.
_Avoid_: tokens, Assistant extra, token pack

**Flagship**:
The Product the homepage leads with. Currently Compliance, followed by Translation Tools.

**Landing page**:
A Product's marketing page on this site: benefit-led copy, pricing, and calls to action. Not documentation and not an info page.
_Avoid_: Product page, info page

**Framework page**:
A page under a Product's Landing page that is about one Framework of Compliance, such as ISO 27001, or about one law kept as separate Frameworks, such as the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR. It is not a Landing page, which stays a Product's marketing page.
_Avoid_: Landing page

**Dutch page**:
A page in Dutch for one Dutch-speaking audience, such as `/nl/gemeenten/` for gemeenten (Dutch municipalities). It is not a translation of an English page: it has no English twin and no `hreflang`. It is not a Landing page, which stays a Product's marketing page, and not a Framework page.
_Avoid_: Dutch Landing page, translated page, localised page

**Resume**:
The pages describing past client and employer work (formerly called "Projects" on the site).
_Avoid_: Projects, portfolio

**Share card**:
How a page looks when someone pastes its link into a feed such as LinkedIn or Slack: the markup in that page's head, plus the 1200 x 630 picture the markup points at. Every page on the site has one.
_Avoid_: link preview, social card, OG image

**mvdmio**:
The company brand the site speaks as. Michiel appears as the founder on the about page, not as the site's voice. The legal trade name of the sole proprietorship is **mvdm.io**.
_Avoid_: treating mvdmio as the KvK name

**mvdm.io**:
The legal trade name of the sole proprietorship that offers the Products (KvK 86594737). Documents name this party, not the brand. In prose a Product carries this name as a prefix on first use in a section — "mvdm.io Statistics" — and stands bare after that.
_Avoid_: mvdmio as the contracting party

**Legal page**:
A public page on this site that publishes one vendor document. Not a Landing page. Each page carries an effective date. A later edit replaces the page; continued use of a Product is acceptance. For a Consumer purchase, mvdm.io emails the buyer instead. A rule that holds for one Product only lives under a Product subheading.
_Avoid_: info page, legal centre

**Terms of Service**:
The one contract that lets a customer use any Product. Written product-neutral, with a subheading for whatever holds for one Product alone.
_Avoid_: Customer Agreement, User Agreement; a separate contract per Product

**Partner terms**:
The Legal page holding the Partner programme's contract: the Commission a Partner earns, payment through Stripe, and the self-billing agreement under which mvdm.io writes the Partner's invoices. Written for Partners, not customers.
_Avoid_: Partner agreement, affiliate terms

**Partner**:
A person or organisation mvdm.io has accepted to bring customers to the Products and earn a Commission. Not a customer reseller and not a Product user by that fact.
_Avoid_: affiliate, reseller

**Referral**:
A customer account tied to one Partner, either because the customer registered through the Partner's link or named the Partner at registration, or because mvdm.io set it.
_Avoid_: lead

**Commission**:
The share of a Referral's paid licence charges that a Partner earns. AI credit packs and trials earn none.
_Avoid_: payout, bonus

**Self-billing invoice**:
The invoice mvdm.io issues in a Partner's name for each Commission payment, so the Partner does not invoice for it.
_Avoid_: Partner invoice

**Privacy Notice**:
The notice of personal data **mvdm.io** processes as controller — site visitors, people who sign in, and billing. It also covers a Partner's billing details and payment records (billing address, tax ID, Commission payments and Self-billing invoices), and names a Partner as a recipient of an account's name and the Commission each invoice earned.
_Avoid_: Privacy Policy

**Data Processing Agreement**:
The contract for personal data a customer keeps in mvdm.io Compliance, mvdm.io Statistics, mvdm.io Health Check, or mvdm.io Translation Tools, which **mvdm.io** processes as processor. mvdm.io Commonplace falls outside it — there mvdm.io is controller.
_Avoid_: using this name for a file a customer attaches to their own supplier

**Subprocessor list**:
The public list of third parties **mvdm.io** uses to process customer data, grouped by application: one section for the providers every Product uses, then one subheading per Product for what only it uses.
_Avoid_: vendors, processors (alone)

**Closed-licence retention**:
The rule that mvdm.io keeps an account's data for a Product for 365 days after that Product's licence ends, then deletes it. A new current licence inside the window stops the countdown.
_Avoid_: grace period, dormancy

**Company details**:
The identification page for the sole proprietorship: legal name, legal form, KvK number, VAT identification number, address, and contact. Every other Legal page repeats this identification word for word.
_Avoid_: Imprint, Colophon, Legal notice, Impressum

**Consumer purchase**:
A Product bought by an individual for themselves rather than by an organisation. mvdm.io Commonplace is the only one, and it carries a 14-day right of withdrawal.
_Avoid_: personal plan, B2C tier
