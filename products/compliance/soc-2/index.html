01

Run SOC 2 in Compliance

Compliance has a SOC 2 catalog based on the 2017 Trust Services Criteria with the 2022 revised points of focus. It holds the Common Criteria for Security and the criteria for Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality and Privacy. CC6.1, for example, covers logical access security.

Every SOC 2 report includes the Common Criteria. You pick the other categories for your report scope. On the Applicability page you mark each criterion Applicable or Excluded.

You link evidence, findings and register records to the criteria they meet.

A CPA firm examines your controls and issues the SOC 2 report. SOC 2 is an attestation report, not a certification. Compliance is where you keep the evidence, findings and records the CPA firm asks to see.

02

Your frameworks overlap. Your work doesn't have to.

Crosswalk mappings link related requirements across the frameworks you adopt. Evidence, findings and register records can meet requirements in several frameworks.

One access control record, for example, meets ISO 27001 A.5.15, SOC 2 CC6.1 and GDPR Art. 32. The crosswalk marks SOC 2 CC6.1 and ISO 27001 A.5.15 as equivalent, and GDPR Art. 32 as related to both. SOC 2 CC8.1 and ISO 27001 A.8.32 on change management are marked equivalent as well.

Your SOC 2 examination and your ISO 27001 audit stay separate, because each one covers its own framework. A supplier review covers one supplier, and it does not span frameworks either.

03

Shared registers, risks and review tasks

Compliance has registers for policies, risks, assets, suppliers, evidence and findings, among others. The registers are shared between the frameworks you adopt, so SOC 2 and ISO 27001 use the same records.

Compliance scores each risk on a 5×5 likelihood and impact matrix. Treatment actions lower the residual risk score.

Compliance creates recurring review tasks for policies, risks, assets and requirements on schedule. Every task has an owner and a due date, and all tasks appear in one inbox.

Compliance also has an AI assistant on every page. It runs on AI credits, billed in USD, and it is not part of the trial unless you buy AI credits.

Can I reuse my ISO 27001 work for SOC 2?

Yes. Crosswalk mappings link related requirements across the frameworks you adopt, so one access control record meets ISO 27001 A.5.15, SOC 2 CC6.1 and GDPR Art. 32.
