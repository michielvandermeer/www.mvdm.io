01

Every register in one place

Not a folder of documents — a living management system where every record knows what it covers.

  • Policies, risks, controls, assets, evidence, audits, vulnerabilities, incidents, continuity and training — thirteen registers, one system
  • Risks sit on a 5×5 likelihood/impact matrix, with treatment actions that drive the residual score down
  • Evidence, audits and findings link straight to the requirements they prove or fail
02

Frameworks that share your work

Frameworks overlap enormously. Compliance maps that overlap for you, so adding a framework is a delta on what you've already done — not a second program.

  • Ships with ten framework catalogs: ISO 27001:2022, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001 (2015 and 2026 editions), ISO 42001:2023, SOC 2, Cyber Essentials, EU GDPR, UK GDPR and the EU AI Act
  • Crosswalk mappings connect matching requirements across every catalog you've adopted
  • Coverage tallies show exactly what is satisfied and what is left to do, per framework
03

An AI assistant that does the legwork

On every page, an assistant that knows your records — not a chatbot bolted on the side.

  • Ask it anything about your ISMS; it reads your actual registers, not a generic script
  • It drafts policies, files risks and updates records for you — but completing a task, cancelling one, or deleting a record always waits for your confirmation first
  • Import your existing documentation and let it map what you already have onto your registers
04

Tasks that keep you certified

Certification is not a project — it is a rhythm. The task layer turns every recurring obligation into scheduled, owned work.

  • Recurring reviews for policies, risks, assets and requirements, spawned automatically on schedule
  • Every task has an owner and a due date, gathered in one cross-cutting inbox
  • Templates for the ad-hoc work you repeat every cycle, on your own cadence
