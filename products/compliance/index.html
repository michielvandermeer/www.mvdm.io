01

Every register in one place

Compliance keeps your management system in registers. Policies, risks and evidence each have one, and so do findings and incidents.

  • Compliance has registers for policies, risks, assets, people, suppliers, evidence, findings, vulnerabilities, incidents, continuity and training, among others.
  • Compliance scores each risk on a 5×5 likelihood and impact matrix. Treatment actions lower the residual risk score.
  • Evidence, audits and findings link straight to the requirements they prove or fail
02

Frameworks that share your work

Frameworks often ask for the same control in different words. Compliance maps those requirements to each other. You keep one access control policy and one set of evidence, and ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR each show it against their own requirement.

  • Compliance has catalogs for ISO 27001:2022, ISO 9001 in the 2015 and 2026 editions, ISO 14001 in the 2015 and 2026 editions, ISO 42001:2023, SOC 2, BIO2 2026, Dutch Arbo 1998, Cyber Essentials, EU GDPR, UK GDPR and the EU AI Act.
  • Crosswalk mappings connect matching requirements across every catalog you've adopted
  • Each framework shows what is satisfied and what is still open.
  • Dutch municipalities that track BIO2 and the GDPR can read BIO2 en AVG voor gemeenten, a page in Dutch.
03

An AI assistant on every page

The assistant reads your registers and answers questions about your own records.

  • Ask it anything about your ISMS; it reads your actual registers, not a generic script
  • It drafts policies, files risks and updates records. It asks you to confirm before it completes a task, cancels one or deletes a record.
  • Import your existing documentation and let it map what you already have onto your registers
04

Recurring work with an owner and a due date

Compliance turns each recurring obligation into a task. Every task has an owner and a due date, and every open task appears in one inbox.

  • Compliance creates recurring reviews for policies, risks, assets and requirements on a schedule.
  • Templates for the ad-hoc work you repeat every cycle, on your own cadence

Questions buyers ask

Which frameworks does Compliance support?

Compliance supports ISO 27001, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 42001, SOC 2, BIO2, Dutch Arbo, EU GDPR, UK GDPR, the EU AI Act and Cyber Essentials. You adopt the ones you need and run them side by side from one set of records.

What does Compliance cost?

Compliance is billed per framework, per year, excluding VAT. ISO 27001, ISO 9001, SOC 2 and BIO2 cost €999 each. ISO 14001 and ISO 42001 cost €749 each. EU GDPR, UK GDPR, the EU AI Act, Cyber Essentials and Dutch Arbo cost €199 each. Users are unlimited. Each framework includes AI credit every year, $10 or $5, billed in USD.

How does the free trial work?

Try Compliance for 30 days without a credit card. The trial gives you full access to every framework. The AI assistant is not part of the trial unless you buy AI credits.

How does billing work if I adopt several frameworks?

You are billed per year for each framework you adopt, and you can adopt any framework. The fees add up. For example, ISO 27001 and EU GDPR together cost €999 plus €199 a year, excluding VAT.

Is there a limit on users?

No. Compliance has unlimited users in your organisation.

Who is Compliance for?

Compliance is for organisations that run ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR or other frameworks side by side. Pricing is per framework, and users are unlimited.

Can one record meet requirements in several frameworks?

Yes. Crosswalk mappings connect matching requirements across the frameworks you adopt. One access control record, for example, satisfies ISO 27001 A.5.15, SOC 2 CC6.1 and GDPR Art. 32. One piece of evidence, one finding or one register record can meet requirements in several frameworks. An audit still covers one standard, and a supplier review covers one supplier. Each framework shows what is satisfied and what is still open.
