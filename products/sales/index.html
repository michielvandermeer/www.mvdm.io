01

Forms you paste into any site

A Form is the sign-up box on your page. You build it in Sales and paste it into the site you already have, whatever that site runs on.

  • A person gives their email address, and their name and company if you ask for them
  • Each Form shows a consent text the person ticks before they sign up, so you keep a record of what they agreed to
  • Use as many Forms as you like, on as many pages and sites as you like
02

Every Lead keeps its UTM campaign

You always know what brought a Lead in, because the page they signed up on and its UTM campaign are saved with them.

  • Add ?utm_campaign= to a link, and every Lead who signs up from it keeps that UTM campaign
  • Export your Leads whenever you need them somewhere else
  • Erase a Lead when they ask, and their data is gone
03

Hosted in the EU, with no new vendor

Your Leads are people's personal data, so where they live matters. Sales is hosted in the EU, and it runs on the same four Subprocessors as every other mvdmio Product.

  • If you already have a Data Processing Agreement with mvdm.io, Sales adds no new Subprocessor to your own DPA
  • The four are Hetzner, Cloudflare, Stripe and Sentry, and the Subprocessors page lists what each one does
