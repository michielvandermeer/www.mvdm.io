# Changelog

## 2026-10-31: The Commonplace page describes the Journal
The Commonplace page now opens its numbered sections with one about the Journal: a dated entry that starts on today's date and the town you're in, keeps only the place name and never your coordinates, can be searched by text or place, and holds images and remarks. The sections that followed moved from 01-04 to 02-05. The Journal is now also named in the hero line, the row of labels under the hero, the Plain pricing box and the page description. The homepage's Commonplace card and llms.txt name the Journal too. Nothing else changed.

## 2026-10-31: Four Landing pages mention the 30-day trial
The Commonplace, Translation Tools, Health Check and Statistics pages now say a 30-day trial exists. A note under the hero buttons and a note under the buttons of the Plain pricing box say you can try the Product for 30 days without a credit card. The pricing note adds that access closes when the trial ends and nothing is charged unless you subscribe. Nothing else on the pages changed.

## 2026-10-30: Privacy Notice names every cookie and browser storage item
Section 4 of the Privacy Notice now lists every cookie the apps set, in two groups: the cookies needed for the service, and the cookies that remember a choice you made. It also lists the two items the apps keep in the browser's local storage. For each one it gives the purpose and how long it lasts. Before, the page said the sign-in cookie was the only cookie mvdm.io sets. Nothing else in the notice changed.

## 2026-10-30: Framework pages link each other
The eight Framework pages (ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 42001, EU AI Act and Cyber Essentials) now carry an "Other frameworks in Compliance" section before the FAQ. It lists links to the other seven Framework pages, so a reader can move from one Framework to another without going back to the Compliance page. Nothing else changed.

## 2026-10-30: Legal pages say which providers get what, and what their terms say about training
Section 8 of the Terms of Service and section 10 of the Data Processing Agreement now name xAI, TypeSafe and Anthropic with what each one receives, and say what the providers' published terms say about training: they do not use that content to train models. Before, both pages said mvdm.io does not promise that. The Subprocessor list now names every use, adds xAI and Anthropic under Commonplace and TypeSafe under Compliance, and links each provider's data processing agreement where one exists. Section 9 of the Data Processing Agreement lists TypeSafe. The Privacy Notice has a new mvdm.io Commonplace part in section 6, covering who receives journal content, where it goes, how long it stays and who at mvdm.io can read it, and section 9 now names the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. The Terms have a new section on Commonplace Allowance packs, which the withdrawal terms link to, and the Commonplace page now says the Assistant runs on packs bought separately. The four legal pages carry the date 30 October 2026.

## 2026-10-29: Search-engine data ties every Product to mvdmio
The search-engine data of the five Landing pages, the eight Framework pages and the Dutch page now names mvdmio as the publisher of every Product. The homepage data now also describes mvdmio itself (legal name, logo, address, VAT number, Chamber of Commerce number and the GitHub organisation) and points at its founder, and the About page now carries data describing the founder, with the founder's LinkedIn profile. No visible page text changed.

## 2026-10-28: Data Processing Agreement names the transfer basis for each United States subprocessor
Section 9 of the Data Processing Agreement now says, for each United States subprocessor, what its own data processing agreement with mvdm.io relies on. The table gained a Transfer basis column: Stripe, Cloudflare and Sentry rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the standard contractual clauses the European Commission adopted in 2021 as the fallback; xAI and Anthropic rely on those clauses, Module Two or Three, and the row for Anthropic says that mvdm.io contracts with Anthropic Ireland, Limited; the Hetzner row says no transfer. The sentence under the table, which used to say only "their own standard contractual clauses", now names mvdm.io as data exporter and the provider as data importer, says what covers UK personal data, links the Commission decision, and offers a Customer a copy of the clauses on request. Qualys SSL Labs no longer appears in section 9, because it processes none of mvdm.io's data. The provider agreements and the Data Privacy Framework List were checked on 28 October 2026. The Data Processing Agreement keeps the date 28 October 2026.

## 2026-10-28: Framework pages link the public demo
The eight Framework pages (ISO 27001, ISO 42001, EU AI Act, SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and Cyber Essentials) now carry a "Try the demo" button in the hero, between Get started and Contact me. It links the public Compliance demo. The rest of the pages is unchanged.
