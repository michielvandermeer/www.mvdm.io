# Changelog

## 2026-10-21: Compliance pages no longer say "every framework" outside the trial line
The headings of the register section on the ISO 42001 and EU AI Act pages now read "Shared registers, risks and review tasks". The answer on one record and several frameworks on the Compliance page, in the page and in its search-engine data, now says mappings connect requirements across the frameworks you adopt. The ISO 27001 page now says a record can meet requirements in the other frameworks it maps to. The trial line, which says the trial gives full access to every framework, is unchanged.

## 2026-10-21: Data Processing Agreement promises breach notification within 48 hours
Section 11 of the Data Processing Agreement now says mvdm.io will notify the Customer of a personal-data breach without undue delay, and within 48 hours, after becoming aware of it. Before, it gave no number of hours. The rest of the agreement is unchanged, and the page now carries the effective date 21 October 2026.

## 2026-10-20: Homepage and Compliance descriptions no longer call Compliance certification-ready
The descriptions of the homepage and the Compliance page, in search results and in link previews, and the description in the Compliance page's search-engine data no longer call Compliance certification-ready or count its frameworks. They now describe it as a management system for running frameworks and name them.

## 2026-10-20: Compliance wording matches the application
The ISO 27001 page now says Compliance scores each risk on a 5×5 likelihood and impact matrix and that treatment actions lower the residual risk score, in the page and in its answer about risks. The lists of registers on the ISO 27001 page, the Compliance page and the homepage no longer name controls and audits, which Compliance has no pages for. The llms.txt file no longer calls Compliance certification-ready or counts its frameworks, and names them instead.

## 2026-10-19: Compliance has a Dutch page for gemeenten
Compliance now has a page in Dutch for gemeenten at /nl/gemeenten/. It explains how BIO2 and the AVG are tracked side by side, with the BIO2 and AVG fees, the free trial and a list of questions gemeenten ask. The Compliance page links to it from the section about frameworks that share your work.

## 2026-10-19: Compliance page and homepage wording changed
The Compliance page heading now reads "ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR compliance software", and the line under it opens with "Your frameworks overlap. Your work doesn't have to." The body, the trial note, the register, framework, assistant and task sections, the pricing table, the closing block and four answers in Questions buyers ask are reworded in plainer language. Each pricing row now says how much AI credit a framework includes and that it is billed in USD, and the answer on one record and several frameworks says an audit still covers one standard. On the homepage, the hero, the price line and the three Compliance cards use the same wording, and the framework strip now lists all eleven frameworks, including BIO2 and Dutch Arbo. The arrows in captions and links are gone.

## 2026-10-19: Homepage and Compliance wording no longer imply one audit covers every framework
The homepage hero now says the evidence and registers you keep up once are reused for every framework they apply to, and no longer says audits or findings are shared. The Compliance page's question and answer, the hero label and the homepage card now say one record can meet requirements in several frameworks, and that a record satisfies a control. No counting language remains.

## 2026-10-18: Fonts served from mvdm.io
Every page now serves its own typefaces from mvdm.io instead of loading them from Google, so opening a page no longer sends your address to Google, and text appears at once instead of waiting for Google's font stylesheet. The pages look the same. The Privacy Notice loses its Google Fonts section, its remaining sections are renumbered 1 to 10, and it carries the date 18 October 2026.

## 2026-10-18: Compliance page lists BIO2 and Dutch Arbo, with questions answered
The Compliance page now lists BIO2 and Dutch Arbo with the other frameworks, in the hero, the framework catalogs and the pricing table. It also has a new section, Questions buyers ask, that answers common questions about frameworks, cost, the free trial and billing.

## 2026-10-07: Logs kept at most 90 days
Section 7 of the Data Processing Agreement now says that application logs, error reports, and traces are kept for at most 90 days. The old line said logs were kept for 7 to 14 days, which did not count the error reports and logs that go to Sentry. The limit covers mvdmio's own server and Sentry alike, and the agreement still carries the date 7 October 2026.

## 2026-10-07: Sentry added to the Subprocessor list
The Subprocessor list now names Sentry, which receives error reports, traces and logs from every product. Sentry is a company in the United States that stores this data in Germany, and the old note that said error tracking runs on mvdmio's own machine is gone. Section 9 of the Data Processing Agreement now shows a table with the country of each subprocessor's company and the country where it stores the data, and both pages carry the date 7 October 2026.

## 2026-09-16: Shared links show a picture
Sharing a page from this site now shows a picture instead of a bare link. The picture carries that page's own heading and the colour of the product it belongs to. Every page has one, including the five product pages, the blog posts, the resume entries, the legal documents, and the page you land on when an address is wrong.

## 2026-08-30: Footer dashes sit between items
The dash between items in the footer is no longer part of a link. It is not underlined, and clicking it does not open a page or a mail message. The email and the Legal page names still open the same destinations.

## 2026-08-30: Quieter header and footer
The header now has two rows. Open source, Blog, About, and Resume stay visible, and the five product names plus Get started sit below, behind the menu on a phone. The footer lists mvdmio, who built the site, the email, and the five Legal pages.

## 2026-08-29: Legal PDFs contain only the document
A Legal page PDF now contains only that document: the title, the effective date, and the body. Printing the page from the browser gives the same file. Download PDF, the site navigation, and the website footer stay on the screen pages.

## 2026-08-29: Legal pack ready to download
You can download each Legal page as a PDF, or one zip of all five, from the legal index. The wording in the PDF is the wording on the page.

## 2026-08-29: Legal pages cover every product
The Terms of Service, Privacy Notice, Data Processing Agreement and Subprocessor list now cover all five products rather than Compliance alone. Every legal page repeats the same company identification, and the subprocessor list is grouped by product so you only read the entries for what you bought. The pages also describe how the applications actually work: hosting is one server in Nuremberg, Germany, and the data behind a closed licence is kept for 365 days before it is deleted.

## 2026-08-28: Legal pages in the footer
Every page footer now links Terms of Service, Privacy Notice, Data Processing Agreement, Subprocessor list, and Company details. Those pages are public and listed in the sitemap.

## 2026-08-24: Compliance billed per Framework
Compliance is now billed per Framework. The Landing page lists three annual fee bands with included AI credit; the homepage Flagship line leads with the €999 band for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and ISO 9001.

## 2026-08-15: New site built around the products
The site has a completely new design that leads with what mvdmio sells. Each product — Compliance, Translation Tools, Health Check, Statistics, and Commonplace — now has its own landing page with clear pricing, and the homepage points straight to them. The blog, resume, and about pages keep their old addresses, and every page loads faster because the site is now plain pages with no machinery behind them.
