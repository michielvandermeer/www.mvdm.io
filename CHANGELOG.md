# Changelog

## 2026-08-24: Compliance billed per framework
Compliance is now priced per framework, not as one organisation licence. The Landing page lists three annual fee bands with included AI credit; the homepage Flagship line leads with the €999 band for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and ISO 9001.

## 2026-08-15: New site built around the products
The site has a completely new design that leads with what mvdmio sells. Each product — Compliance, Translation Tools, Health Check, Statistics, and Commonplace — now has its own landing page with clear pricing, and the homepage points straight to them. The blog, resume, and about pages keep their old addresses, and every page loads faster because the site is now plain pages with no machinery behind them.
