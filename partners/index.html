I build, you sell, we both profit

I have spent my working life as a software developer. Building is the part I am good at. I took over an information security management system at Jewel Software and saw how much of the work was still done by hand. I built the Compliance Platform to take that work off people's plates.

I have always wanted a company with no employees. AI finally makes that possible, because I can hand a lot of the work to bots. Winning customers is still the part I cannot hand over. Marketing is not my strength, and I am not as good at steering marketing bots as I am at steering development bots. I also do not want to spend my days on the phone. That is not shyness. I just want to build.

So I am looking for the other side of that split. A one-person marketing or sales specialist who loves to sell and does not love to build. You get a product that keeps improving fast, direct support from the builder, and a Commission of 20% of the licence charges each of your Referrals pays for any Product, calculated on the amount without VAT and after any discount, for as long as the Referral stands.

What you sell

The Compliance Platform is an online information security management system (ISMS). It is EU-hosted by default. It is delivered empty, so a client can import an existing ISMS or start with the AI assistant. They can also connect the AI they already use, through the Compliance MCP at https://compliance.mvdm.io/agents.

Public prices, excluding VAT:

  • ISO 27001, ISO 9001, SOC 2, and BIO2 are €999 per framework per year.
  • ISO 14001 and ISO 42001 are €749 per framework per year.
  • EU GDPR, UK GDPR, the EU AI Act, Cyber Essentials, and Dutch Arbo are €199 per framework per year.

A client can start with a trial of 30 days and no card. Trials earn no Commission, because they are not paid. AI credit packs earn no Commission.

What you earn

If you become a Partner, you earn a Commission of 20% of the licence charges each of your Referrals pays for any Product. The 20% is calculated on the amount without VAT and after any discount. It applies to every paid invoice from the moment the Referral is set, including renewals, for as long as the Referral stands.

On an undiscounted ISO 27001 licence of €999 a year, that is €199.80 a year, for as long as that client stays your Referral.

You keep any fee you charge for your own sales or advisory work. The client pays mvdm.io for the licence. Commission is held for 30 days after the customer's invoice is paid, then paid once a month, on or after the 1st, through Stripe, as the Partner terms describe.

A customer becomes your Referral when they register through your Partner link, or when they pick you at the registration question "Did a Partner send you?".

How to become a Partner

mvdm.io decides who becomes a Partner. There is no apply form. Write to michiel@mvdm.io and tell me what kind of buyers you already reach. If I offer you the partnership, you accept by replying to that email, and I will send you a Partner link.

The demo is at https://demo.mvdm.io/compliance. The Partner terms are at https://mvdm.io/legal/partners/. The Compliance Platform is at https://mvdm.io/products/compliance.

This is also for consultancies

The same program is open to ISO consultants, security consultancies, and similar firms. If you already sit with the client and implement an ISMS, you keep your fees, and you earn the same Commission of 20% of the licence charges each of your Referrals pays for any Product, for as long as the Referral stands. The product starts empty, so you can bring your own method.

If that is you, write to the same address and say what kind of clients you help.
